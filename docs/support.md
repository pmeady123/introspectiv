## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I create a note?
Tap the "+" button on the home screen to create a new note. You can write in plain text or use markdown formatting.

### What is Incognito mode?
Notes marked as Incognito are stored only on your device and are never uploaded to our servers or used for matching.

### How does matching work?
When you mark a note as eligible for matching, we create a semantic embedding (a mathematical representation of the meaning). We then find other users who have written about similar topics. Both users must accept the match before any content is revealed.

### How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. This will permanently remove all your data from our servers.

### Is my data secure?
Yes. We use encryption in transit and at rest, row-level security policies, and your notes are only shared when you explicitly consent to a match.

### How do I report a user?
In any chat conversation, tap the menu icon and select "Report User". Our team will review the report promptly.

## App Not Working?

Try these steps:
1. Force close and reopen the app
2. Check your internet connection
3. Update to the latest version from the App Store
4. If issues persist, contact us at the email above
