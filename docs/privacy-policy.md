# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 3, 2025**

## Introduction

Introspectiv ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Introspectiv mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide

- **Account Information**: When you create an account, we collect your email address and authentication credentials.
- **Notes and Content**: The notes, text, and images you create within the App.
- **Profile Information**: Your generated persona handle and any preferences you set.

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers.
- **Usage Data**: How you interact with the App, including features used and timestamps.
- **Biometric Authentication**: If enabled, biometric data is processed locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

### AI-Generated Data

- **Semantic Embeddings**: We use OpenAI's embedding technology to create mathematical representations of your notes' meaning. These embeddings are used solely for the matching feature and cannot be reverse-engineered to recreate your original text.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide and maintain the App's core functionality
- Enable the note-matching feature to connect you with users who share similar thoughts
- Generate anonymous match suggestions and conversation prompts
- Improve and optimize the App experience
- Communicate with you about updates and support
- Enforce our Terms of Service and protect against misuse

## The Matching Feature

Our matching feature connects users based on the semantic similarity of their notes:

- Only notes you explicitly mark as eligible for matching are processed
- Notes marked as "Incognito" are never uploaded, vectorized, or shared
- Matches require mutual consent before any content is revealed
- You interact through anonymous personas, not real identities
- You can block or report users at any time

## Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following circumstances:

### With Other Users
- When you mutually accept a match, the matched notes become visible to the other user
- Chat messages are shared only with your matched conversation partner

### With Service Providers
- **Supabase**: Cloud database and authentication services
- **OpenAI**: AI processing for semantic embeddings (note content is processed but not stored by OpenAI)
- **Expo**: Push notification delivery

### Legal Requirements
We may disclose information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests.

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including:

- Encryption in transit and at rest
- Row-level security policies on our database
- Secure authentication protocols
- Optional biometric app lock

## Data Retention

- Your notes and account data are retained while your account is active
- You can delete individual notes at any time
- Account deletion removes all associated data from our servers
- Backup data may persist for up to 30 days after deletion

## Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Correction**: Update inaccurate information
- **Deletion**: Delete your account and associated data
- **Incognito Mode**: Prevent specific notes from being processed or shared
- **Opt-Out**: Disable matching features while retaining note functionality

To exercise these rights, contact us at patrick.m.eady@gmail.com.

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.
